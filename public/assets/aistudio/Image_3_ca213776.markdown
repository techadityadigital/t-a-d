## Brand & Style

Tech Aditya Digital exudes a high-end modern digital marketing aesthetic tailored for ambitious brands. The design system is engineered to deliver a sleek, immersive dark mode experience that immediately commands attention and communicates premium expertise. 

### Brand Personality & Target Audience
The brand personality is authoritative, cutting-edge, and culturally resonant. It targets discerning founders, marketing directors, and enterprise clients seeking high-impact digital growth. The tone is confident, sophisticated, and results-driven.

### Emotional Response & Design Style
The UI evokes a sense of exclusivity, electric energy, and refined luxury. Drawing from a refined **Glassmorphism** and **High-Contrast / Bold** hybrid style, the interface relies on deep slate foundations contrasted with luminous amber-gold accents that capture a sophisticated Diwali and festive spirit. Emerald green acts as an intentional conversion catalyst, guiding user focus toward high-value actions and direct messaging channels.

## Colors

The color architecture is built on a deep slate canvas designed to make luminous accents vibrate with energy. 

- **Primary (Amber/Gold):** `#F59E0B` serves as the core brand beacon, used for primary interactive states, glowing borders, and focal typography.
- **Secondary (Emerald Green):** `#10B981` is reserved strictly for conversion touchpoints, success states, and WhatsApp integrations.
- **Tertiary (Electric Blue):** `#3B82F6` provides analytical contrast and data visualization accents.
- **Neutral (Slate):** Built on `#0F172A` (Slate-900) for the primary canvas and `#1E293B` (Slate-800) for elevated surface layers and cards.

## Typography

Typography balances geometric futurism with exceptional digital readability. **Space Grotesk** drives headlines with sharp, technological precision, while **Plus Jakarta Sans** ensures conversational clarity across all body copy and UI labels. 

On mobile viewports, scale down `headline-xl` to 36px and `headline-lg` to 28px to maintain proportional balance and prevent awkward line wrapping. Ensure all body copy maintains high contrast against the slate backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system employs a **Fluid 12-column grid** layout that scales seamlessly from mobile devices to ultrawide displays. 

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing is anchored on a versatile 4px/8px incremental scale. Generous whitespace is utilized to separate high-value marketing modules, preventing cognitive overload and reinforcing the high-end digital agency aesthetic.

### Responsive Behavior
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacking with 1rem canvas margins and condensed gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid adaptation with 1.5rem margins.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Maximum 12-column layout constrained to a 1280px container with 2rem outer margins and 1.5rem column gutters.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is achieved through **Ambient shadows** paired with subtle **Glassmorphism** layers. Because the interface exists entirely within a dark mode paradigm, standard drop shadows are insufficient. 

Instead, use multi-layered box shadows with low-opacity amber or slate-tinted glows (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)` for interactive focal points). Surface layers utilize semi-transparent Slate-800 fills with 1px frosted borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) to establish clear hierarchy without relying on harsh lines.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded** shape language (`roundedleness` level 2), featuring 0.5rem base border radius for standard components, scaling to 1rem (`rounded-lg`) for cards and containers, and pill-shaped variants for badges and conversion triggers. This creates an approachable yet precise silhouette that feels modern and ergonomic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Amber/Gold background (`#F59E0B`) with Slate-900 text, featuring a subtle outer glow on hover.
- **Conversion / WhatsApp:** Emerald Green (`#10B981`) background with pure white text, designated for direct messaging and primary conversion actions.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent fill with a 1px Slate-700 border and light text.

### Chips & Badges
Pill-shaped components utilizing soft tinted backgrounds (e.g., Amber-900/40 for festive tags, Emerald-900/40 for success indicators) paired with matching high-contrast label text.

### Input Fields
Built on Slate-800 surfaces with 1px Slate-700 borders. On focus, the border transitions smoothly to Amber/Gold with a soft, diffused glow.

### Cards
Constructed using Slate-800 backgrounds (`#1E293B`) with frosted borders. Interactive cards feature an upward translational shift and intensified ambient glow on hover.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
Custom styled with Slate-800 wells and Amber/Gold checked states, utilizing smooth micro-animations.

### Additional Agency Components
- **Metric Callouts:** Large Space Grotesk statistical displays paired with trend indicators.
- **Festive Banner Modules:** Special promotional containers featuring subtle radial gradients of warm amber and deep slate.
- **Direct-Connect Floating Action Button (FAB):** Fixed-position WhatsApp launcher utilizing the Emerald Green palette with a persistent pulse animation.
